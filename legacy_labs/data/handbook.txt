TECHCORP EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK 2026

Section 1: Vacation Policy
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year.
PTO does not roll over to the next year. You must use it by December 31st.

Section 2: Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days a week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are 
mandatory in-office days for team collaboration.
